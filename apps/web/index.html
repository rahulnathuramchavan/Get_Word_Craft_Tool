WordCraft Tool — Find Better Words, Solve Puzzles Faster

WordCraft Tool is a free, fast, privacy-focused word-game utility and reference toolkit. Built on an open 437,673-word English ENABLE dictionary, our tools help you unscramble letter racks, solve anagrams, crack tough crossword clues, rescue your Wordle streak, and look up definitions — with zero registration, zero accounts, and no sign-up required.

Eight Fast, Free Word-Game Tools

  • Word Finder

    Search for words using rack letters, blank wildcards (?), and board patterns to find tournament-legal plays.

  • Word Unscrambler

    Unscramble any jumbled rack of letters into high-scoring words sorted by length or classic Scrabble score.

  • Anagram Solver

    Find every exact anagram permutation for jumbles and puzzle clues in milliseconds.

  • Wordle Helper

    Filter valid 5-letter words using green (confirmed), yellow (misplaced), and gray (excluded) letter constraints.

  • Crossword Finder

    Solve crossword puzzle blanks by matching known letters and blank wildcard dots (e.g. c..ft).

  • Scramble Generator

    Generate guaranteed-solvable anagram scrambles for vocabulary training and puzzle creation.

  • Definition Finder

    Instant English dictionary definitions, parts of speech, and audio pronunciations.

  • Word Lists

    Browse curated lists of 2-letter words, high-scoring words, Q-without-U words, and vowel-heavy words.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is WordCraft Tool really free?
Yes. Every tool is 100% free with no subscription, paywall, or account requirement.
Which word list is used?
We use the public-domain ENABLE dictionary of over 437,673 words, paired with a common-English frequency layer.
Do you store my searches or data?
No. All searches execute client-side in your browser. We never log or store search terms.
